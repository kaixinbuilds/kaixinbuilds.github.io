Lino prints (dottyprints) used on the site.

DO NOT put Instagram screenshots here. They carry app chrome, like counts and
other people's profile photos, and they are too low-resolution to print well
on a retina screen. Export the original image instead, or re-photograph or
scan the print itself.

What each file should be:
  - the artwork only, cropped to the paper edge, nothing else in frame
  - 1600px on the long side is plenty
  - PNG for flat scans, JPEG for photographs of a print
  - under ~400KB each
